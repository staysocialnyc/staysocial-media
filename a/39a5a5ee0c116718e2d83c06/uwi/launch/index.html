Plan v2 in 30 seconds

  • The lane is open: no pan-Caribbean meme page sells real merch. Caribbean pages engage about 5x more than big mainstream ones (partly because they're smaller).
  • Copy the winners' structure, not their jokes: one look, one sign-off, named series, fan submissions, an always-on store with dated drops.
  • Content: mostly single text cards like @justinlaboy, family framing first, short captions, morning + evening posts.
  • Merch: drop 1 around Barbados' 60th (Nov 26–30), drop 2 for Carnival season (Jan 14–Feb 28). Winning posts become shirts.
  • Always learning: six department agents, a Saturday trend brief, 2 experiments a week, one Monday report for you.
