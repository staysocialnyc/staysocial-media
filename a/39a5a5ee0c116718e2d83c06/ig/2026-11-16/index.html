Sorrel needs two to three days to steep. Start by Monday and it's ready for Thanksgiving 🌺

Every Caribbean auntie knows the red drink doesn't happen overnight. The classic method:

1️⃣ Boil it with ginger and spices
2️⃣ Steep it for two to three days
3️⃣ Add sugar, wine or rum

And the story in the cup:
🌺 Sorrel is brewed from roselle, a tropical hibiscus. It's the same plant behind bissap in many African countries
🌺 Food historians tie it to red drinks that crossed the Atlantic during the slave trade. In Jamaica, it became sorrel
🌺 It's a Christmas drink for a practical reason: the plant was historically only available around Christmastime
🌺 The same hibiscus pours as agua de Jamaica in Latinx kitchens
🌺 Caribbean immigrants carried sorrel to US cities starting in the 1930s

Sources: WLRN
