Jamaica's national fruit was banned in the US for decades. Here's why 🇯🇲

🍈 Ackee isn't native to Jamaica — it came from West Africa, probably on a slave ship
🍈 Its scientific name, Blighia sapida, honors Capt. Bligh, who took ackee plants from Jamaica to England in 1793
🍈 It's the national fruit and half of the national favorite: ackee and saltfish
🍈 Pick it only after the pod opens on its own — unripe ackee contains a toxin, hypoglycin A
🍈 The US banned canned ackee imports in 1973; shipments from approved processors only started again in 2000
🍈 Jamaicans know two kinds: soft yellow 'butter' ackee and firm, cream-colored 'cheese' ackee

Sources: Jamaica Information Service, USDA Agricultural Research Service

A 19th-century Harlem firehouse is now home to a Caribbean cultural center — and its Carnival exhibit closes Nov 21 🎭

🔥 The Caribbean Cultural Center African Diaspora Institute (CCCADI) was founded in 1976 by Dr. Marta Moreno Vega — 2026 marks 50 years, and Harlem Week honored the anniversary this year
🔥 Its home at 120 E 125th St is a Romanesque Revival firehouse built in 1888–89, used by the FDNY until 2003 and a city landmark since 1997
🔥 CCCADI opened in the restored firehouse in October 2016, after starting out on East 87th Street

📍 CCCADI Firehouse, 120 E 125th St. The gallery is open Tue 12–6 PM, Wed 12–8 PM and Thu 12–6 PM through Nov 21 — that's only a few more visiting days. Free.

The steelpan was once banned as a 'noisy instrument'. Now the UN gives it its own day 🇹🇹

🥁 Before pan came tamboo bamboo — bamboo cut to different lengths; 'tamboo' from the French 'tambour'
🥁 About six years before 1951, Trinidad's Legislative Council banned playing 'noisy instruments' (steelpans) in public
🥁 On July 26, 1951, TASPO played the Festival of Britain in London — the first band with every pan made from oil drums
🥁 TASPO's pans were tuned to concert pitch; Spree Simon and Ellie Mannette were members
🥁 In July 2023, the UN named Aug 11 World Steelpan Day, noting the instrument originated in Trinidad and Tobago
🥁 In July 2024, T&T's Parliament unanimously passed a bill making the steelpan the national musical instrument

NYC's own chapter: Brooklyn's Panorama steelband competition plays the back lot of the Brooklyn Museum during Labor Day Carnival week 🗽

Sources: Oxford Business Group, Caribbean Beat, United Nations, CARICOM, Afropop

Do you know your Kadooment from your Junkanoo? Save this before next season 🌴

🇬🇩 J'ouvert — 'daybreak': Spicemas's early-morning street parade on Carnival Monday
🇹🇹 Panorama — Trinidad & Tobago's big steelband competition, started in 1963; finals land the Saturday before Carnival
🇧🇧 Crop Over — began as a celebration of the end of the sugar-cane harvest
🇧🇧 Kadooment — the finale of Crop Over: masquerade bands parade from the National Stadium to Spring Garden Highway
🇬🇩 Jab Jab — J'ouvert masqueraders covered in black oil or charcoal, wearing horned helmets and carrying chains
🇧🇸 Junkanoo — Boxing Day and New Year's morning parades on Nassau's Bay Street, with goatskin drums and cowbells

And Brooklyn keeps its own chapter: our J'ouvert moves at first light down Flatbush Ave every Labor Day 🗽

Sources: Pure Grenada, Oxford Business Group, Caribbean Beat, NCF Barbados, Bahamas.com, Library of Congress
