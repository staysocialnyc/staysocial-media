Queens has a Little Guyana, and the A train says so 🇬🇾 Save this for a Sunday trip:

🇬🇾 Richmond Hill, Queens is home to the largest Guyanese population in New York City
🇬🇾 Queens' Guyanese community is called the largest outside Guyana, and Guyanese are NYC's fifth-largest foreign-born group
🇬🇾 Guyanese families have shaped Richmond Hill and Ozone Park since the 1970s and 80s
🇬🇾 On May 29, 2021, the corner of Liberty Avenue and Lefferts Boulevard was co-named 'Little Guyana Avenue'
🇬🇾 In March 2024 the MTA unveiled a 'Welcome to Little Guyana' plaque inside the Lefferts Blvd A-train station, and conductors are encouraged to announce the stop that way

📍 The route: ride the A train to its Lefferts Blvd end, find the plaque in the station mezzanine, then walk Liberty Avenue's Guyanese-owned shops to the Little Guyana Avenue sign at the corner of Lefferts Blvd.

Sources: NYC Council (Caribbean Life), Queens Daily Eagle
